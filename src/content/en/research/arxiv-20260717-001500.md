> **Original paper:** [Open-AoE: Open-source All-in-one of Egocentrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14183)

## Overview

Open-AoE is an open-source egocentric manipulation dataset and toolchain that covers the full workflow from smartphone video collection to model training. Its first release contains roughly **2,000 hours of real-world manipulation video**, contributed by more than **500 participants** using over **400 smartphones** in natural environments.

The project addresses a persistent bottleneck in embodied intelligence: collecting diverse, scalable, and sufficiently structured data for learning general-purpose manipulation skills.

## Core Innovations

### 1. A Scalable Data-Collection Model

Conventional robot datasets often depend on expensive laboratory hardware and tightly controlled collection procedures. Open-AoE instead uses ordinary smartphones and a community-contribution workflow. This substantially reduces the marginal cost of gathering demonstrations while increasing diversity across people, objects, tasks, and environments.

### 2. Multi-Layer Structured Annotations

The dataset provides several complementary annotation layers:

- Natural-language descriptions of manipulation activities
- MANO-based hand pose and shape parameters
- Egocentric camera trajectories
- Temporally localized atomic actions

Together, these annotations support research that connects visual understanding, hand motion reconstruction, action segmentation, and robot policy learning.

### 3. An End-to-End Toolchain

Open-AoE is more than a video archive. Its tooling covers temporal action segmentation, semantic labeling, hand reconstruction, camera trajectory estimation, visualization, cross-embodiment retargeting, and model-specific data conversion. The release also includes training recipes for vision-language-action policies, World Action Models, and World Models.

## Potential Industry Value

For universities and smaller robotics companies, the open collection workflow and standardized processing tools can reduce the cost of building useful embodied-AI datasets. Cross-embodiment retargeting may also make it easier to transfer knowledge from human demonstrations to humanoid robots and dexterous manipulation systems.

At a broader level, the dataset offers large-scale natural-environment observations for training models that must predict how actions change the physical world. This could accelerate progress toward more adaptable robotic systems that learn from human activity rather than relying exclusively on robot-collected demonstrations.

## Outlook

Open-AoE presents a practical model for combining low-cost collection, community participation, structured annotation, and reusable tooling. If its coverage and quality continue to grow, it could become useful public infrastructure for embodied learning and general-purpose robotic manipulation.
