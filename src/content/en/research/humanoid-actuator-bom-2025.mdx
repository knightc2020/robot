## Background

The core cost of humanoid robots is concentrated in joint actuator modules. This report systematically breaks down the full-body joint actuator BOM cost based on in-depth interviews with 12 core suppliers worldwide.

## Key Findings

- Full-body actuator BOM cost per unit: **$6,200 - $9,400** (depending on DOF count)
- Harmonic drives account for **35-42%** of per-joint cost
- Chinese domestic alternatives have reduced certain joint costs by **20-30%**
