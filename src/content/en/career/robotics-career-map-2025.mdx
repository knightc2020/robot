## Data Notes

Based on 210 anonymous survey responses collected in Q3-Q4 2025, covering North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific markets.

## Salary Overview

| Specialization | Entry Level ($/yr) | Mid-Level ($/yr) | Senior ($/yr) |
|---------------|-------------------|------------------|---------------|
| Mechanical Design | 75-95k | 95-130k | 130-170k |
| Embedded Systems | 85-110k | 110-150k | 150-200k |
| Motion Control | 90-120k | 120-160k | 160-220k |
| SLAM/Perception | 100-130k | 130-180k | 180-250k |
| RL/AI | 110-140k | 140-200k | 200-280k |

## Key Insights

- RL/AI commands the highest premium but positions are limited
- Embedded systems offers the best risk-adjusted entry point
- Remote-friendly roles are expanding in SLAM/perception
